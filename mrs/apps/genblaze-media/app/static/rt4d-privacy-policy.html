When you use the Service, we may automatically collect information about your interactions with it, including:

  • technical error information;
  • device, browser, network, or IP address information made available through standard server logs; and
  • information relating to generated previews, including file identifiers, integrity hashes, and rendering metadata.

User-Submitted Content

The Service may process prompts, scene descriptions, scene settings, rotations, projection values, and other information that you submit for the purpose of creating, updating, inspecting, or rendering a scene.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the right to submit any information or content provided to the Service.

We may use collected information to:

  • provide, operate, and maintain the Service;

  • diagnose errors and respond to support requests;
  • monitor performance, reliability, abuse, and security;
  • enforce applicable terms, policies, and legal requirements;

You should not submit confidential, sensitive, regulated, or personally identifiable information in scene prompts unless you are authorized to do so and accept the associated processing risks.
