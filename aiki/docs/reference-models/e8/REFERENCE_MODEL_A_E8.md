## What E₈ is (established math)

E₈ is the unique even unimodular lattice in eight dimensions; root lattice of the E₈ Lie algebra. Cited properties in the source note include:

- Optimal sphere packing in 8D (Viazovska 2016)
- 240 minimal root vectors
- Energy optimality for broad functionals among configurations in ℝ⁸
- Clean even/unimodular harmonic structure

**Scope caution (from source):** these properties do **not** imply that cognitive or governance systems “naturally converge” to E₈. The lattice is an **existence proof** of highly symmetric constrained geometry, used as a worked example.

E₈ is **Reference Model A** — the first documented mathematical reference for governed state geometry under constitutional neutrality. It illustrates the separation pattern. Future models may replace or sit alongside it if evidence supports them.
